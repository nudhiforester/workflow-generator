# Login Page
1. Layout & Background:
   - Full-screen layout with a 50/50 vertical split background: bright yellow (#FFCC00) on the left side and solid pitch black (#000000) on the right side.
   - Centered floating glassmorphism container placed directly over the background split line.

2. Outer Glass Overlay Card:
   - Rounded corners (border-radius: 24px) with a thin black border stroke.
   - Dark semi-transparent background overlay with backdrop-blur.
   - Text elements centered at the top: Bold header "Welcome" in black, followed by a subtitle "Enter your email and password to log in".

3. Inner Form Card:
   - Solid white background card centered inside the glass container with generous padding and rounded corners (border-radius: 12px).

4. Form Controls & Typography:
   - Email field: Top label "Email", rounded border input with light gray placeholder "Enter Your Email".
   - Password field: Top label "Password", rounded border input with light gray placeholder "Enter Your Password".
   - Primary CTA Button: Full width, solid yellow background (#FFCC00) matching the left side background, thin black border outline, black text "Sign In", rounded corners.
   - Helper Text: Small gray caption centered below the button reading "Read the documentation to understand how access credentials are created".
